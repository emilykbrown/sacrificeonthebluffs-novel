So I think your research database should track cases across several dimensions: what happened; victim and relationship to perpetrator; disability; original charge; plea/conviction; sentence; statutory sentencing range; prosecution argument; defense argument; judge's stated reasoning; evidence of caregiver stress/support availability; media framing; disability-community response; and whether disability/burden/mercy language actually affected the legal outcome.
That last distinction matters enormously. A short sentence alone doesn't prove ableism.
