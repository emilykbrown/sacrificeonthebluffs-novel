Save the Cat Main Elements

Genre: 

A Story: Hannah, a woman with Cerebral Palsy, uncovers the dark secrets of her small town after her best friend goes missing. As she delves deeper, she realizes that the town’s dark secrets are rooted in a pervasive belief that disabled lives are worth less, leading to sinister practices that even implicate the ones she loves the most.

B Story: In the wake of her best friend’s disappearance, Hannah struggles with internalized ableism, believing she must handle everything on her own to prove her worth. As she confronts the town's dark secrets, she learns that accepting help and relying on those who truly love her doesn’t make her weak; it allows her to fight back stronger against the forces that devalue her life and the lives of others like her.
